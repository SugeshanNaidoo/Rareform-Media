About

Two people, one studio, no middlemen.

Coastline Creative started because we kept seeing good businesses let down by the way they looked online. We decided to do something about it.

Our story

It began with a frustration

Between us we'd worked on enough shoots and campaigns to notice a pattern: the businesses doing genuinely good work were often the ones with the worst photos, the emptiest social accounts and the website that hadn't been touched since 2019. Meanwhile competitors with a fraction of the substance were winning on presentation alone.

That felt solvable. So we set up on our own, kept the team to two, and built a studio around one idea — that presentation should match substance, and that a small business shouldn't need an agency retainer to get there.

Today we work with restaurants, trades, retailers and service businesses across Durban and the surrounding coast. Some come to us for a single shoot. Others hand over their whole online presence. Both are fine by us.

What we believe

The rules we work by

Finish it properly

Nothing leaves here at eighty percent. If a shot isn't right we go back out; if a layout feels off we redo it. That's the whole job.

Say it plainly

You'll never get a proposal full of language designed to make simple work sound complicated. Clear scope, clear pricing, clear timelines.

Learn the business first

We ask a lot of questions before anyone picks up a camera. Content that ignores how a business actually makes money tends to look nice and do nothing.

Keep getting better

New gear, new techniques, new platforms. What worked eighteen months ago rarely still does, and we'd rather chase that than coast on it.
