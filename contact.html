Questions

Answered before you ask

The things people most often want to know before getting in touch.

What does a project cost?

It depends entirely on scope, so there's no honest single number to put here. A logo and a basic brand kit sits at the lower end; ongoing content with paid advertising sits well above it. Send us a rough brief and we'll come back with a written quote and a fixed scope.

How long does it take?

Design work and edits usually land within one to two weeks of the shoot or brief. Websites take longer — typically three to five weeks depending on how many pages there are and how quickly copy and images come through from your side.

Do you travel outside Durban?

Yes. We cover the KwaZulu-Natal coast as standard, and we'll travel further for larger projects. Anything outside the immediate area may carry a travel cost, which we'll always put in the quote rather than adding it afterwards.

What do you need from us to start?

An idea of what you're trying to achieve, any brand assets you already have, and a rough budget range. If you don't have brand assets, that's fine — that's often the first thing we build.

How many rounds of changes do I get?

One full round of revisions is included on every project. In practice we rarely stop there — if something isn't sitting right, we'd rather fix it than sign it off.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, 50% up front to lock in the dates and start work, with the balance due on delivery. For monthly retainers, we invoice at the start of each month.

Who owns the final files?

You do. Once the project is paid up, the finished photos, videos and design files are yours to use however you like. We'll only feature the work in our own portfolio if you're happy for us to.

Can we start small and add more later?

That's how most of our clients work. Plenty begin with a single shoot or a logo and move onto a retainer once they've seen how it goes. There's no obligation either way.
